# Acoustic Mechanics, Stealth & Explosive Distractions

## 1. Overview & Tactical Philosophy
Subterranean fauna in *Voidfall: Dredge* hunt through acoustic and seismic vibration sensing rather than purely visual sightlines. High-frequency drilling, heavy footsteps, and weapon discharges generate distinct sound signatures that travel through cavernous air and strata.

Players can exploit this acoustic sensitivity using tactical distractions:
- Deployable concussive demolition charges that draw predators away from extraction points or extraction routes.
- Ballistic impacts and structural collapses that trigger localized tremors, shifting swarm attention to remote rockfaces.

---

## 2. Acoustic Hierarchy & Salience Calculation
Rather than treating distance uniformly, enemies compute **Acoustic Salience** to prioritize high-energy, explosive disturbances over faint nearby sounds:

$$\text{Salience} = \frac{\text{Intensity} \times M_{\text{type}} \times (\text{LOS} \ ? \ 1.0 : 0.65)}{\max(\text{Distance}, 1.0)}$$

### Sound Taxonomy & Salience Multipliers

| Sound Type | Base Radius / Intensity | Salience Multiplier ($M_{\text{type}}$) | Generated By | Behavioral Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Demolition Blast** | $35.0\,\text{m}$ | **$3.0\times$ - $3.5\times$** | Remote Demolition Charge Detonation | Supreme distraction; overrides stalking player |
| **Seismic Tremor** | $25.0\,\text{m}$ | **$3.5\times$** | Natural tremors or Ballistic Voxel Shattering | Triggers subterranean burrower navigation |
| **Drilling / Mining** | $12.0\,\text{m}$ | **$1.0\times$** | Power Drill extraction | Gradual predator accumulation |
| **Gunfire / Weapon Discharge**| $22.0\,\text{m}$ | **$1.8\times$** | Plasma Carbine, Needler Railgun, Scattergun | Immediate detection in line of sight |
| **Bullet Impact** | $10.0\,\text{m}$ | **$1.8\times$** | Projectile striking rock or bulkhead | Draws stalkers toward ricochet point |
| **Voxel Break** | $8.0\,\text{m}$ | **$1.2\times$** | Block destruction | Mild alert pulse |
| **Sprinting Footsteps** | $8.0\,\text{m}$ | **$1.0\times$** | Uncrouched movement | Alerts nearby prowlers |
| **Crouched Footsteps** | $2.5\,\text{m}$ | **$0.4\times$** | Stealth crouch walking | Near-silent movement |

## 3. Tactical Demolition Satchel Charges & Remote Detonator
Delvers equipped with `ToolSlot::DemolitionCharge` hold an industrial **Tactical Remote Detonator** clacker equipped with an illuminated LCD telemetry display, antenna mast, safety guard arches, and tactile push plunger:

1. **Deployment (LMB)**:
   - Targets any solid voxel face within reach ($7.5\,\text{m}$).
   - Anchors a physical 3D PBR-lit Satchel Charge model to the targeted voxel face (dual explosive blocks, steel retention brackets, hazard chevron stripes, and pulsing strobe beacon).
   - Deducts 1 charge from inventory (`m_inventory.demolition_charges--`).
   - The handheld Detonator's display updates to an armed pulsing red/amber indicator (`ARMED: SATCHEL LINKED`).

2. **Detonation (RMB)**:
   - Wirelessly triggers the deployed charge from safe distance via the handheld detonator plunger.
   - Depresses the physical plunger switch on the viewmodel with tactile click travel.
   - The planted satchel charge instantly detonates, blasts a crater, shatters creatures, kicks up dust, and vanishes from the cavern wall.
   - Generates a heavy concussive explosion:
     - Blasts nearby weak voxels within a $3.0\,\text{m}$ crater radius.
     - Emits a massive acoustic signature (`DemolitionBlast`, intensity $35.0\,\text{m}$).
     - Emits secondary seismic shockwaves (`SeismicTremor`, intensity $25.0\,\text{m}$).
     - Deals kinetic damage to subterranean burrowers and hostile entities in the blast volume.
     - Kicks up dust particle clouds and micro camera trauma.

## 4. Ballistic Block Damage & Localized Tremors
Projectiles from player firearms (Plasma Carbine, Needler Railgun, Magma Scattergun) interact physically with cavern architecture:

- **Ballistic Damage Accumulation**:
  - Each hit damages the target voxel using the lower 4 bits of `Voxel::flags_and_damage` ($0\dots15$).
  - Needler Railgun deals $15$ damage (instant shatter).
  - Plasma Carbine deals $6$ damage per round ($3$ rounds to shatter).
  - Magma Scattergun deals $4$ damage per pellet.
- **Structural Shattering**:
  - When voxel damage reaches $\ge 15$, the block collapses into air (`MAT_AIR`).
  - Triggers voxel break sounds, block fragment debris particles, and resource item drops if minable.
- **Localized Seismic Tremors**:
  - Ballistic shattering ruptures surrounding cavern strata, emitting an acoustic `SeismicTremor` pulse ($18.0\,\text{m}$ radius).
  - Nearby monsters (Void Stalkers, Burrowers, Swarms) register the tremor at the impact coordinates and investigate.

## 5. Enemy Behavioral Responses

### Void Stalkers (`void_stalker.cpp`)
- **Stalking State Distraction**:
  - If a stalker is stalking the player and hears a `DemolitionBlast`, `SeismicTremor`, or `BulletImpact` at a distance $>4.5\,\text{m}$ from the player, it breaks off pursuit.
  - Transitions immediately to `StalkerState::Investigating`, locks the explosion coordinates as its target, and sets `investigation_timer = 5.0s`.
- **Circling State Distraction**:
  - Circling stalkers break orbit and prioritize investigating distant blast points, allowing trapped players to slip past choke points.

### Seismic Burrowers (`seismic_burrower.cpp`)
- Highly sensitive to seismic pulses.
- Salience calculation weighs `DemolitionBlast` and `SeismicTremor` by $3.5\times$.
- When triggered during `Burrowing` or `Breaching`, the burrower re-routes its subterranean path toward the blast epicenter.

## 6. Overhead Enemy Awareness Indicators & Diegetic Sensory Feedback

To maintain dark subterranean tension and minimize screen clutter, intrusive on-screen text banners (such as acoustic disturbance or predator tracking notifications) have been replaced by in-world visual awareness indicators and 3D spatialized audio cues:

1. **Acoustic Suspicion / Investigating Mode (Yellow `!` Marker)**:
   - When a hostile organism (Void Stalker or Seismic Burrower) registers an acoustic disturbance or is in `Investigating` / `Stunned` state:
     - A floating yellow badge containing an exclamation mark (`!`) appears above the creature's head.
     - Features a high-contrast dark slate pill backing, electric safety yellow border, downward indicator caret, gentle vertical bobbing, and a pop-in scale spring animation.
     - If occluded behind solid rock walls, the marker is rendered at half opacity ($0.55\alpha$), providing the player with tactical acoustic awareness of hostiles lurking behind strata.
     - Accompanied by a 3D spatialized alien chittering / clicking acoustic cue (`SoundCue::StalkerChitter`) emitted directly from the investigating organism.

2. **Engaged / Combat Mode (Red Triangle `▲`/`▼` Marker)**:
   - When an enemy spots the player and enters active pursuit (`Stalking`, `Circling`, `Lunging`, or ranged shooter line-of-sight aiming; Burrower `Breaching`, `Charging`, `Enraged`):
     - The marker transforms into a throbbing Crimson Red triangle pointing down toward the hostile's head with an inner dark core and alert mark.
     - Rapid tactical combat pulse ($12\,\text{Hz}$) alerts the player to immediate danger.
     - Accompanied by spatialized spotting chitter/growl (`SoundCue::StalkerSpotted`) or predatory leaping screeches (`SoundCue::StalkerLunge`), replacing redundant HUD text warnings.

3. **Unalerted / Ambient State**:
   - Prowling or idle creatures display no overhead indicators, preserving uncompromised stealth immersion in the cavern depths.

## 7. Delver Stealth Stance & Sneak Attack Critical Hits

### 7.1 Sneaking / Crouching Mechanics (`Ctrl` Key)
Holding `Left Ctrl` engages Delver Stealth Stance, drastically altering subterranean acoustics and enemy perception:
- **Acoustic Dampening (`NoiseMeter`)**:
  - Footstep noise intensity is reduced by **$-65\%$** (`STEALTH_SOUND_INTENSITY_MUL = 0.35f`).
  - Physical audible radius in cavern strata is reduced by **$-55\%$** (`STEALTH_SOUND_RADIUS_MUL = 0.45f`).
  - Footstep sounds during crouch walking become virtually imperceptible beyond $2.5\,\text{m}$.
  - The HUD Acoustic Meter displays `ACOUSTIC PROFILE // STEALTH (SUPPRESSED -65%)` in cyan/amber.
- **Visual Concealment**:
  - Delver optical detection radius in dark shadows drops from $10.0\,\text{m}$ to $5.2\,\text{m}$.

### 7.2 Enemy Alertness Dissipation
Subterranean fauna investigate acoustic disturbances, but delver stealth directly dissipates suspicion:
- When an enemy (Void Stalker or Seismic Burrower) is in `Investigating` state:
  - If the player remains crouched and silent, enemy investigation suspicion drains **$2.5\times$ faster** (`s.investigation_timer -= dt * 2.5f`).
  - Suspicion subsides in ~1.4 seconds of sustained stealth, returning the creature to `Idle` / `Unalerted` state without engaging combat.
  - If line-of-sight is broken while in `Stalking` state, crouching breaks enemy tracking within $2.0\,\text{s}$ (down from $4.5\,\text{s}$).

### 7.3 Sneak Attack Critical Hits ($3.0\times$ Damage)
Striking an unalerted enemy (`is_unalerted() == true` — Idle, with no active pursuit or investigation targets) inflicts a devastating sneak attack critical strike:
- **Damage Multiplier**: Deals **$3.0\times$ base kinetic / plasma / drill damage** (`SNEAK_ATTACK_CRIT_MULTIPLIER = 3.0f`).
- **Critical Hit Marker**:
  - Crosshair renders an expanded radiant golden-amber/crimson critical reticle ($0.35\,\text{s}$ duration) featuring outer barbed chevron prongs, a central flash diamond, and floating `CRIT!` text.
- **Audio Feedback (`SoundCue::CritHit`)**:
  - Procedurally synthesized punch combining a downward frequency transient snap ($2800\,\text{Hz} \to 900\,\text{Hz}$), a deep $90\,\text{Hz}$ kinetic sub-punch, dual harmonic crystal chimes ($1480\,\text{Hz}$ / $2220\,\text{Hz}$), and a vital structural fracture crunch.
- **HUD Feedback**:
  - Displays tactical notification: `<< SNEAK ATTACK CRITICAL HIT (3.0x DAMAGE) >>`.
- **Alert Transition**:
  - Surviving targets are immediately provoked into pursuit, preventing repeated sneak attacks on engaged targets.
